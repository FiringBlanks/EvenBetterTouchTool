# Hardware acceptance checklist

Automated tests use synthetic contact frames and command plans. They do **not** prove physical trackpad recognition, accessibility hierarchy, media delivery, native conflict suppression, software audio support, TCC permissions, login startup, or idle CPU. Record OS version, device ID, and observations when running these checks.

1. Run `./install.sh --dry-run`, inspect the legacy daemon list, then install using `./install.sh`. Confirm the original same-user CLI process is gone and its LaunchAgent is archived under `~/.config/threefinger-migration/`. Confirm one EvenBetterTouchTool app process and one menu bar icon. Start a second copy: it must not create another engine.
2. Keep a mouse or keyboard available. Verify the menu bar disable and Control–Option–Command–Escape. If the hotkey was already registered by another app, diagnostics must warn and menu bar disable remains the recovery path.
3. Grant the **installed** app Accessibility and Input Monitoring; quit/reopen. In Setup Assistant manually configure the listed native gesture settings. No native settings should change merely from opening the app or checking a confirmation.
4. First leave calibration empty: confirm readiness has no calibration blocker and Sensitivity/gesture editors/Diagnostics show % units. Test continuous tab/volume movement, stationary contacts, swipe reversal, and tap/swipe separation. Calibration is optional. For physical-distance tests, in Calibration verify the selected device and expand Precision calibration. Use a ruler and one finger to measure 30 mm on X, then Y; lift between trials. Repeat each axis and verify scale consistency. Alternatively measure the active surface width/height, enter them, click Use Measurements, and Save. Invalid dimensions show why they cannot be used; there is no confirmation toggle. Confirm Reset discards edits and Use Relative Sensitivity stays pending until Save. Confirm calibration applies only to that device. Custom actions must pause during the wizard.
5. Open Chrome with 15 tabs. Use Diagnostics to check contact counts, coordinates, recognized direction, and action time. Move three fingers 10 mm horizontally: expect 10 switches at the default 1 mm interval. Hold still for two seconds: no more switches. Move 3 mm back: expect three switches in the opposite direction. Test 0.5 and 20 mm thresholds. A brief first motion within the 40 ms landing window should count once the axis locks.
6. Open Finder with at least three tabs **in the current window**. Repeat both directions. A separate Finder window, a single-tab window, and the desktop must not be switched by the action. If the app reports no eligible window despite tabs, save diagnostic output and investigate AXTabGroup hierarchy before loosening the eligibility guard.
7. An unprofiled app must initially receive no custom three-finger horizontal actions. Add its profile, assign an explicit shortcut, change its sensitivity, and verify global fallback, per-app overrides, disabled cells, and disabled profiles independently. Switch apps while touching: lift to rearm; no old action should arrive in the new app.
8. For each swipe: test correct count, diagonal rejection, an extra finger after the landing window, one finger lifting early, replacement fingers, and fast/noisy movement. Swipes must never fire tap actions on release. Reversals must follow movement without waiting for a new session. No movement must mean no new action.
9. Three-finger taps: test Play/Pause while Music, Spotify, and a browser media session are active, including while another app is foreground. Verify it behaves like the dedicated media key. Test next/previous-track mappings. Pause all players and test no active session.
10. Confirm the default three-finger Click mapping is enabled for Play/Pause; enable and Save it if testing an older saved configuration. Rest three fingers on a blank area, physically press, then release: expect one media toggle plus the normal mouse click. Mere touch/lift must do nothing when Tap is disabled. Repeat with four-finger mappings, fast/slow presses, normal clicks, wrong finger counts, physical drags, Force Touch, and an external mouse while fingers rest on the pad. The latter may trigger by design; document any workflow conflict. Verify click cooldown, late landing, freshness, motion/hold limits, app overrides, source filtering, and disable/Reset behavior. Synthetic generated mouse events are ignored by default. Physical presses must never also fire a Tap mapping.
11. Verify Look Up, ordinary tap clicks, three-finger drag, Mission Control, and App Exposé do not fire alongside custom gestures after manual setup. This app cannot suppress those features if they remain enabled. Four-finger horizontal swipes should switch desktops **once natively**, with no synthetic action in diagnostics. For custom horizontal reassignment, first disable the native setting and confirm the separate gate.
12. Four-finger vertical: move 12 mm up at default 4 mm/2%: volume should increase 6 percentage points. Hold still: no changes. Reverse 4 mm: decrease 2 points. Check top/bottom clamping and four-finger tap mute. Switch between built-in speakers, headphones, and a device without software volume; unsupported devices must show a useful diagnostic, not crash or change unrelated devices.
13. Verify Mission Control/App Exposé configured keyboard shortcuts and optional fallbacks. Change a shortcut in System Settings and test again. Disabled/unreadable OS settings without a confirmed fallback must not generate assumed Control–Arrow events.
14. Test each action you intend to use: screenshot UI, Lock Screen (save work first), app activation/open, browser navigation, left/right/middle click, custom modifier combinations, and ordered shortcut sequences. Change foreground apps midway through a sequence: remaining keys must cancel. No sequence may overlap another.
15. Revoke Accessibility: no synthetic actions should occur. Revoke Input Monitoring: custom actions must stop. Restore permissions and relaunch if macOS requires it. Verify the dashboard reflects actual grants rather than inferring permission from device count.
16. Sleep/wake, lock/unlock, and disconnect/reconnect external trackpads. Verify exactly one callback/engine resumes. A disconnected selected device must not silently use another trackpad's calibration. Unknown-ID devices must use relative units until optionally calibrated again.
17. Edit sensitivities, setup confirmations, profile switches, row switches, and login startup: verify nothing applies or changes the saved file until Save. Reset must restore saved values. Edit a gesture, then close with X, Escape, and an outside click: all must discard the edit. Gesture Save must persist only that cell; other edits must remain pending. Import and Restore Defaults must stay pending until Save; Save must create a backup. Export must contain saved values. Edit JSON externally with pending drafts: verify the Reset/review conflict flow. Quit with unsaved edits and reopen: only saved settings should remain. Invalid external JSON must report an error and pause actions while retaining the file.
18. Toggle Start at Login in the installed app; inspect System Settings → General → Login Items, log out/in, and confirm one instance without a permanent Dock icon. Turn startup off before uninstall. Run the provided uninstall script; confirm no app/login item remains and configuration/backups remain. Restore native gestures manually.
19. Measure idle CPU in Activity Monitor for five minutes, then exercise continuous gestures. Record CPU/memory and event responsiveness. Synthetic timing/cap tests are not hardware performance benchmarks.

20. On a fresh install, verify an EvenBetterTouchTool shortcut appears automatically in the main Applications folder. Launch through it, install again, and verify it still opens the same real app without extra copies. If Applications is unwritable or a conflicting file exists, verify the desktop fallback and preservation of that file. Uninstall through either the canonical app path or the alias path: matching shortcuts should disappear; unrelated aliases/apps and saved settings must remain. The shortcut lifecycle is also covered by automated temporary-fixture tests.

21. Open Settings from Finder, the menu bar, and Command–comma. Confirm the top-left macOS menu says EvenBetterTouchTool, and Settings menu navigation works. Switch apps, return, and Save edits: its menu must remain correct. Command–W closes Settings; menu bar engine stays running and temporary Dock icon disappears with Menu Bar Only saved. Reopening must restore the app menu. Test Quit and normal text editing shortcuts.

22. Open a migrated left/right Sequence with exactly one shortcut: Continuous must be editable. Toggle it, dismiss without Save, and verify nothing changed. Reopen, enable, Save, and verify repetition follows movement, retains fractions, reverses on direction change, and stops when stationary. Add a second shortcut: Continuous must be disabled with an explanation and the grid must show Single. Remove it: Continuous becomes editable again. In an inherited app mapping, choose Customize for This App; edits remain local until Save.

23. Over a long Chrome page, run custom three-finger volume and tab swipes. Verify the page's scroll position stays fixed throughout and after lifting (momentum included). Two-finger scrolling and mouse wheels must still work immediately afterward. Repeat with disabled rows/profiles, a native mapping, emergency pause, another app, stale/reconnected input, and an external smooth-scrolling mouse. Mixed four-finger native up/custom down must keep native up behavior; look specifically for any leading scroll leak before raw direction is available. Do not claim native Mission Control/App Exposé interception.
24. In Diagnostics, test Mission Control, App Exposé, and Show Desktop separately. Verify their macOS Keyboard Shortcuts are enabled and their exact fallbacks are saved if preference entries are missing. Inspect Last action for the sent shortcut or actionable failure. Show Desktop must test F11 if that is the verified setting. Repeat actual gesture delivery and different foreground apps; tests do not prove hardware success.
25. Preview Volume Indicator: it must show 42% without modifying output volume, leave the foreground app unchanged, and close after two seconds. Then adjust actual volume by gesture: compare its number to the macOS volume control, reverse direction, stop, and check dismissal two seconds later. Test clamping, multiple outputs, software-volume-unsupported devices, full-screen apps, spaces, and an open settings window. The indicator is owned by this app, not the macOS Sound menu.

26. Test forgiving taps with 0.4-second touches, staggered landing/lift, and small drift. Test the duration/drift/cancellation boundaries, repeated taps through cooldown, and 3→4 settling. Test a triggered swipe that remains within tap drift: release must never add a tap action. Adjust all fields globally, per app, and per cell; verify Save-only application, Reset, import/export, and old schema decoding. Keep three-finger tap playback disabled when testing deliberate click playback.

27. Adjust volume repeatedly across one/two/three-digit percentages: the feedback panel must stay at its first position, including after menu bar layout settles. Stop for two seconds and observe a short fade. Move the pointer less than 24 logical screen points: it stays visible until the idle deadline. Wiggle repeatedly within that radius: motion must not accumulate. Add/remove fingers, type, click without pointer movement, scroll, switch apps by keyboard, or use another gesture: none starts an early fade. Move the pointer at least 24 points from the last volume update: the fade begins immediately. Continue adjusting volume during a fade: the same panel must revive without moving or being hidden by an old completion. The normal gesture must keep working; monitors do not swallow input. Test native four-finger Mission Control and custom nonvolume actions, full-screen windows, and external displays.
28. In Global Gestures, use each ↕ icon to exchange that column's three/four-finger mappings. Verify action and every parameter move, row toggles remain, and nothing applies until Save. Reset restores the prior state. In an app profile, swap inherited cells and verify new overrides only in that app, then swap back; globals and other apps must stay unchanged. Test disabled cells and unassigned cells. Save, relaunch, and verify persistence. Native gesture conflicts still need their saved setup confirmations.

29. Open four-finger Click → Assigned action: all five categories must be visible without scrolling a long menu, and Navigation/Media/Mouse/Applications/Miscellaneous must expose their complete lists. Pick an action, Reset, and close without Save; verify the saved mapping stays unchanged. During a real volume swipe, the bar should match the new percentage immediately with no delayed fill animation. At a 0.5-unit interval, move slowly across exact interval boundaries and reverse; no completed step should be withheld solely because the final increment is below jitter tolerance. Test rapid changes and an explicit volume cooldown; stationary fingers must cause no repeat. Uncalibrated units remain axis percentages.

30. Show Diagnostics → Preview Volume Indicator, keep the pointer still, and use keyboard/modifier input; the panel remains until its two-second idle deadline. During real volume swipes, lift fingers one at a time and add fingers: only meaningful actual pointer movement can dismiss early. Test a slow deliberate cursor move, short accidental wobble, dragging, Retina/non-Retina displays, negative-origin secondary displays, and volume changes during a fade. Screen-point motion is independent of the %/mm gesture thresholds.
